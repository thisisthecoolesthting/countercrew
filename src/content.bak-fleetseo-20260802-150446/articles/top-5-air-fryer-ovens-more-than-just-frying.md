## Top 5 Air Fryer Ovens: More Than Just Frying  

Air fryer ovens have revolutionized countertop cooking by offering versatility beyond just frying. These appliances combine convection heating with rapid air circulation to crisp food with minimal oil while also baking, roasting, and even dehydrating. If you're looking for a multi-functional upgrade to your kitchen, here are five of the best air fryer ovens available today.

## What to Look for in an Air Fryer Oven  

Before buying, consider:  
- **Capacity:** 5–10 quarts suits 1–2 people; 12+ quarts works for families.  
- **Functions:** Look for models with baking, roasting, and dehydrating if you want versatility.  
- **Ease of cleaning:** Removable trays and non-stick interiors save time.  
- **Noise level:** Some convection fans can be loud—check decibel ratings if noise is a concern.  
- **Wattage:** Higher wattage (1500W+) means faster cooking but higher energy use.  

Whether you prioritize speed, smart features, or space efficiency, today’s air fryer ovens go far beyond just frying—they’re true countertop multitaskers.

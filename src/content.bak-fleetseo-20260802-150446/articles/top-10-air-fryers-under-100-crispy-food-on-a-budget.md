## What to Look for in an Air Fryer Under $100  

When shopping for an air fryer on a budget, consider the following factors to ensure you get the best value:  

1. **Capacity:** Choose a size that fits your household. Smaller capacities (2-4 quarts) are great for individuals or couples, while larger capacities (5-6 quarts) are better for families.  
2. **Wattage:** Higher wattage typically means faster cooking times and crispier results. Look for models with at least 1,400 watts.  
3. **Presets:** Air fryers with preset functions make cooking easier, especially for beginners.  
4. **Ease of Cleaning:** Nonstick, dishwasher-safe baskets and trays simplify cleanup.  
5. **Design:** Compact designs save counter space, while clear cook windows let you monitor your food without opening the fryer.  

With these tips and our top 10 list, you’re sure to find an air fryer that fits your budget and cooking needs. Enjoy crispy, healthy meals without breaking the bank!

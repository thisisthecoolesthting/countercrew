## The 9 Best Bagel Toasters: Perfectly Toasted Every Time  

Bagel toasters are a must-have for bagel enthusiasts who crave that perfect combination of a crispy exterior and a soft, chewy interior. Unlike regular toasters, bagel toasters are designed with specific settings to accommodate the dense texture of bagels. Whether you prefer plain, everything, or cinnamon raisin bagels, these appliances ensure consistent results every time. Here are the nine best bagel toasters to elevate your breakfast game.  

### 1. **Cuisinart CPT-122 2-Slice Compact Plastic Toaster**  
This sleek and compact toaster is a favorite for its affordability and performance. It features a bagel setting that toasts the cut side of the bagel while gently warming the outer side. With six browning levels and a removable crumb tray, it’s easy to customize your toast and keep the appliance clean. Its compact size makes it ideal for smaller kitchens.  

### 2. **Breville BTA730XL Bit More Toaster**  
Breville’s Bit More Toaster offers extra lift and wider slots to accommodate thicker bagels and artisan bread. The bagel setting ensures even toasting, while the “A Bit More” button adds extra time for a crispier finish. Its brushed stainless steel design adds a touch of elegance to any countertop.  

### 3. **KitchenAid KMT3115OB 2-Slice Toaster**  
Known for its durability and stylish design, the KitchenAid KMT3115OB features a bagel setting that toasts the inside of the bagel while gently warming the outside. Its extra-wide slots can handle thicker bagels and bread slices, and the sleek retro design complements modern kitchens.  

### 4. **Hamilton Beach 2-Slice Toaster (22708)**  
This budget-friendly option doesn’t compromise on quality. The Hamilton Beach 22708 includes a bagel setting and seven browning levels for precise control. Its compact design and automatic shutoff feature make it a practical choice for small spaces and busy mornings.  

### 5. **Oster TSSTTVMND1 2-Slice Toaster**  
The Oster TSSTTVMND1 combines affordability with functionality. Its bagel setting ensures even toasting, and the extra-wide slots accommodate thicker bagels and bread slices. The removable crumb tray and sleek stainless steel finish make it a practical and stylish addition to any kitchen.  

### 6. **Black+Decker TR1400SD 4-Slice Toaster**  
For families or bagel lovers who need to toast multiple slices at once, the Black+Decker TR1400SD is a top choice. Its independent controls allow you to toast two or four slices simultaneously, and the bagel setting ensures perfect results every time. The sleek stainless steel design adds a modern touch.  

### 7. **Smeg TSF01 2-Slice Toaster**  
If style is a priority, the Smeg TSF01 delivers with its retro design and vibrant color options. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, it features a bagel setting and six browning levels for precise toasting. Its wide slots accommodate thicker bagels, making it both functional and fashionable.  

### 8. **Dualit Lite 2-Slice Toaster**  
The Dualit Lite is a premium choice for those who value durability and performance. Its bagel setting ensures even toasting, and the extra-wide slots can handle thicker bagels and bread slices. The brushed stainless steel finish and classic design make it a timeless addition to any kitchen.  

### 9. **Krups EA8250 Essential 2-Slice Toaster**  
Krups is known for its reliable kitchen appliances, and the EA8250 is no exception. Its bagel setting toasts the cut side while warming the outer side, and the extra-wide slots accommodate thicker bagels. The sleek stainless steel design and compact size make it a great choice for small kitchens.  

### What to Look for in a Bagel Toaster  
When shopping for a bagel toaster, consider the following features to ensure you get the best appliance for your needs:  

1. **Bagel Setting**: A dedicated bagel setting is essential for toasting the cut side while warming the outer side of the bagel.  
2. **Browning Control**: Look for multiple browning levels to customize your toast to perfection.  
3. **Slot Size**: Extra-wide slots are ideal for thicker bagels and artisan bread.  
4. **Capacity**: Choose a 2-slice toaster for individuals or a 4-slice model for families.  
5. **Ease of Cleaning**: A removable crumb tray makes maintenance simple.  
6. **Design**: Consider the aesthetics and size of the toaster to ensure it complements your kitchen.  

### Conclusion  
Investing in a high-quality bagel toaster can transform your breakfast routine, delivering perfectly toasted bagels every time. Whether you prioritize affordability, style, or functionality, there’s a toaster on this list to suit your needs. Pair your favorite bagel with cream cheese, lox, or jam, and enjoy a gourmet breakfast right at home.

## The 5 Best Electric Kettles for Tea Lovers and Coffee Aficionados  

Electric kettles are a must-have for anyone who enjoys a perfectly brewed cup of tea or coffee. They heat water faster than stovetop kettles, offer precise temperature control, and often include features like keep-warm functions and automatic shutoff. Whether you're a tea connoisseur who needs variable temperatures for delicate leaves or a coffee enthusiast who wants near-boiling water for a French press, there’s an electric kettle for you.  

Here are five of the best electric kettles available today, each excelling in different areas.  

---

## 1. **Cuisinart CPK-17 PerfecTemp Electric Kettle**  
**Best for: Precision temperature control**  

The Cuisinart CPK-17 is a favorite among tea lovers for its six preset temperature settings (160°F–212°F), allowing you to heat water to the ideal temperature for green, white, oolong, or black tea. It also has a "keep warm" function that maintains your selected temperature for 30 minutes.  

**Key Features:**  
- 1.7-liter capacity (enough for 6–7 cups)  
- Stainless steel exterior with a blue LED-lit water window  
- BPA-free plastic interior with a removable filter  
- Automatic shutoff and boil-dry protection  

**Why It Stands Out:**  
The precise temperature control ensures delicate teas aren’t scorched, while the keep-warm function is perfect for multiple servings.  

---

## 2. **Breville BKE820XL Smart Kettle**  
**Best for: Coffee aficionados and serious tea drinkers**  

The Breville Smart Kettle offers five preset temperatures (160°F, 175°F, 185°F, 190°F, and 212°F) and a "hold temperature" feature that maintains heat for up to 20 minutes. Its sleek design and rapid heating make it ideal for pour-over coffee or delicate teas.  

**Key Features:**  
- 1.8-liter capacity  
- Stainless steel construction with a polished finish  
- LCD display showing current and target temperatures  
- Soft-opening lid to prevent splashing  

**Why It Stands Out:**  
The hold temperature function is perfect for brewing multiple cups without reheating, and the LCD display adds a high-tech touch.  

---

## 3. **OXO Brew Adjustable Temperature Pour-Over Kettle**  
**Best for: Pour-over coffee and precise pouring**  

OXO’s kettle is designed with coffee lovers in mind, featuring a gooseneck spout for controlled pouring—essential for pour-over methods like V60 or Chemex. It also has adjustable temperature settings (140°F–212°F) in 1°F increments.  

**Key Features:**  
- 0.9-liter capacity (ideal for single servings)  
- Gooseneck spout for precision pouring  
- Backlit display with real-time temperature tracking  
- 30-minute keep-warm function  

**Why It Stands Out:**  
The gooseneck spout ensures a slow, steady stream of water, crucial for even coffee extraction. The small capacity is perfect for solo brewers.  

---

## 4. **Hamilton Beach 40880 Electric Tea Kettle**  
**Best for: Budget-conscious buyers**  

If you want a reliable electric kettle without breaking the bank, the Hamilton Beach 40880 delivers. It doesn’t have variable temperature settings, but it heats quickly and shuts off automatically when boiling.  

**Key Features:**  
- 1.7-liter capacity  
- Stainless steel construction with a removable mesh filter  
- Cordless design with a 360° power base  
- Boil-dry protection  

**Why It Stands Out:**  
It’s an affordable, no-frills option that still offers fast boiling and safety features. Great for those who just need hot water fast.  

---

## 5. **Zwilling Enfinigy Cool-Touch Electric Kettle**  
**Best for: Safety and sleek design**  

The Zwilling Enfinigy features a cool-touch exterior, making it safe to handle even when boiling. It has five preset temperatures and a keep-warm function, plus a minimalist stainless steel design.  

**Key Features:**  
- 1.5-liter capacity  
- Cool-touch double-wall construction  
- Precise temperature control (160°F–212°F)  
- Automatic shutoff and boil-dry protection  

**Why It Stands Out:**  
The cool-touch design prevents burns, and the double-wall insulation keeps water hot longer.

## What to Look for in an Electric Kettle  

When choosing an electric kettle, consider these factors:  

1. **Temperature Control** – Essential for tea drinkers who need lower temps for delicate leaves.  
2. **Capacity** – Larger kettles (1.7L+) are great for families; smaller (0.9L–1.5L) suits solo drinkers.  
3. **Material** – Stainless steel is durable; glass lets you see the water level but can be fragile.  
4. **Pouring Precision** – Gooseneck spouts are best for pour-over coffee.  
5. **Safety Features** – Auto shutoff and boil-dry protection are must-haves.

Whether you prioritize speed, precision, or affordability, one of these kettles will elevate your tea or coffee routine. Happy brewing!

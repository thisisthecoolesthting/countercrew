## The 12-inch cabinet rule

In most apartments, upper cabinets sit **12 inches deep** above the counter. A coffee maker taller than that belongs on an open shelf — not under the microwave cubby.

We tested drip and single-serve brewers with:

- Carafe or reservoir installed
- Lid fully open for filling
- Drip tray extended

### What to check before you buy

- **Width** with the handle swung out (some “slim” models fail here)
- **Reservoir removal path** — can you pull it forward without hitting the backsplash?
- **Warm plate clearance** — does steam vent straight into the cabinet lip?

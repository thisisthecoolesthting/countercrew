## Best Nespresso Machines for Espresso and Lungo Lovers  

For coffee enthusiasts who crave the perfect espresso or lungo at home, Nespresso machines offer convenience, consistency, and barista-quality results. Whether you prefer intense espresso shots or smoother, longer lungos, choosing the right machine depends on features like brew size options, milk frothing capabilities, and ease of use. Below, we’ve rounded up the best Nespresso machines tailored for espresso and lungo lovers.  

## Best Overall: **Nespresso VertuoPlus**  

The **VertuoPlus** stands out for its versatility, brewing both espresso (1.35 oz) and lungo (5 oz) with ease. Its unique Centrifusion technology spins the coffee capsule at high speed, ensuring optimal extraction and rich crema.  

### Key Features:  
- **Adjustable cup size**: Customize your brew length for espresso or lungo.  
- **Large water tank**: 40 oz capacity reduces frequent refills.  
- **Automatic lid**: Opens and closes for effortless capsule insertion.  
- **Compact design**: Fits neatly on countertops.  

Ideal for those who want a no-fuss machine with consistent results.  

## Best for Milk-Based Drinks: **Nespresso Lattissima One**  

If you love lattes or cappuccinos alongside your espresso, the **Lattissima One** is a top pick. It includes an integrated milk frother for creamy, café-style drinks.  

### Key Features:  
- **One-touch milk frothing**: Prepares hot or cold milk foam instantly.  
- **Espresso and lungo options**: Brews 1.35 oz (espresso) and 3.7 oz (lungo).  
- **Removable milk container**: Easy to clean and refrigerate.  
- **Fast heat-up**: Ready in just 25 seconds.  

Perfect for those who want espresso with the flexibility of milk-based beverages.  

## Best Compact Choice: **Nespresso Essenza Mini**  

For small kitchens, the **Essenza Mini** delivers big flavor in a tiny footprint. It’s one of the most affordable Nespresso machines without sacrificing quality.  

### Key Features:  
- **Two programmable cup sizes**: Espresso (1.35 oz) and lungo (3.7 oz).  
- **Energy-efficient**: Auto-off after 9 minutes of inactivity.  
- **Lightweight design**: Weighs just 5 lbs, ideal for travel or tight spaces.  
- **19-bar pressure pump**: Ensures professional-grade extraction.  

A great entry-level option for espresso purists.  

## Best for Customization: **Nespresso Creatista Plus**  

Serious coffee lovers who want precision will appreciate the **Creatista Plus**. Developed with Breville, it offers unmatched control over espresso and milk texture.  

### Key Features:  
- **Steam wand for milk**: Adjustable temperature and foam consistency.  
- **OLED display**: Guides you through brewing and milk-steaming settings.  
- **Eight espresso sizes**: From ristretto (0.5 oz) to lungo (5 oz).  
- **Stainless steel build**: Durable and sleek.  

Ideal for those who enjoy experimenting with coffee recipes.  

## Best Budget-Friendly: **Nespresso Inissia**  

The **Inissia** is a reliable, no-nonsense machine that delivers excellent espresso and lungo at an affordable price.  

### Key Features:  
- **19-bar pressure system**: Rich, aromatic coffee every time.  
- **Fast heat-up**: Ready in 25 seconds.  
- **Lightweight and portable**: Easy to move or store.  
- **Two cup sizes**: Espresso (1.35 oz) and lungo (3.7 oz).  

A solid choice for beginners or those who prioritize simplicity.  

## What to Look for in a Nespresso Machine  

When choosing a Nespresso machine for espresso and lungo, consider these factors:  

1. **Brew Sizes**: Ensure the machine offers both espresso (1-1.5 oz) and lungo (3-5 oz) options.  
2. **Milk Frothing**: If you enjoy milk-based drinks, look for models with built-in frothers or steam wands.  
3. **Capsule Compatibility**: OriginalLine machines work with a wider variety of third-party capsules, while VertuoLine offers larger drink sizes.  
4. **Size and Design**: Compact models save space, but larger tanks reduce refills.  
5. **Ease of Cleaning**: Removable parts and self-cleaning functions simplify maintenance.  

## Final Thoughts  

Whether you prioritize convenience, milk frothing, or customization, there’s a Nespresso machine to match your needs. The **VertuoPlus** is our top pick for its versatility, while the **Lattissima One** excels for milk-based drinks. Budget-conscious buyers will love the **Inissia**, and espresso purists should consider the **Creatista Plus** for precision brewing. Whichever you choose, you’ll enjoy café-quality coffee at home with minimal effort.

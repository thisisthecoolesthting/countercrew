## The Counter Crew measurement card

Before we review any appliance, we fill out the same four-line card:

| Field | How we measure |
| --- | --- |
| **Width** | Widest point, handles included |
| **Depth** | Counter edge to rear vent |
| **Height** | Closed lid + 2 in vent clearance |
| **Cord** | Exit side and usable reach to outlet |

### Cabinet and door swing

Open upper cabinet doors above the target spot. If a door lip hangs lower than the appliance height, the item fails — even if the spec sheet says it fits.

### Write it on the box

Photograph your measurement card next to the appliance when it arrives. Returns are easier when you can show the box depth was wrong, not your counter.

## Start with the footprint, not the quart rating

Most air fryer reviews lead with basket size. On a 24-inch counter between the stove and wall, **depth and height matter more**. We measured three compact models with a steel rule — cabinet lip to counter edge — and noted vent clearance.

### What we measured

- **Width** at the widest point (handles folded in)
- **Depth** from counter edge to back panel
- **Height** with basket closed, plus 2 inches for heat vent clearance

### Quick picks for tight counters

1. **Under 12 inches deep** — fits most galley layouts without blocking the stove knob zone.
2. **Basket under 4 quarts** — enough for two servings without a footprint that rivals a microwave.
3. **Cord exit side** — right-hand outlets hate left-exit cords; we note this in every review.

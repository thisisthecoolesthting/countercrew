## Outlet spacing is the hidden spec

Manufacturers list toaster width. They rarely list **cord pivot** or **lever travel** when the unit sits between two outlets.

### Our measurement checklist

1. Mark the center of each outlet box on the backsplash.
2. Measure clear width between faceplates — not just tile edge to tile edge.
3. Add 1 inch for heat clearance on each side.

### Long-slot vs standard-slot tradeoffs

Long-slot toasters save width but add depth. On counters less than 20 inches deep, we prefer standard slots with a narrow chassis and top-lift levers that do not hit the cabinet.

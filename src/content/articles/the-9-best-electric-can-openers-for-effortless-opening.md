## What to Look for in an Electric Can Opener  

When choosing an electric can opener, consider the following factors to ensure you select the best model for your needs:  

1. **Ease of Use**: Look for models with one-touch operation or hands-free designs for added convenience.  
2. **Safety Features**: Smooth-edge openers or magnetic lid lifters can reduce the risk of injury.  
3. **Durability**: Stainless steel or heavy-duty plastic construction ensures long-lasting performance.  
4. **Additional Features**: Built-in knife sharpeners or bottle openers add versatility to the appliance.  
5. **Size and Storage**: Compact designs are ideal for small kitchens or limited counter space.  
6. **Power Source**: Cordless models offer portability, while corded models provide consistent power.  

By considering these factors, you can find an electric can opener that meets your needs and enhances your kitchen experience.

Squeezing fresh citrus juice elevates everything from morning orange juice to weeknight margaritas. But with so many options for citrus juicers on the market, how do you choose the right one for your needs? We've spent the last month testing both manual and electric citrus juicers, evaluating them on ease of use, juice yield, cleaning, and overall durability. Here’s what we found.
---
## Manual Citrus Juicers: Simple and Reliable

Manual citrus juicers are a classic choice for a reason. They're typically more affordable than electric models, require no electricity, and are easy to store. We tested several types, including handheld reamers, press juicers, and lever-style juicers.

### Handheld Reamers: The Budget-Friendly Option

Handheld reamers are the simplest and most affordable type of citrus juicer. They consist of a ridged cone that you press and twist the citrus fruit against.

**Pros:**

*   **Affordable:** Reamers typically cost under $15.
*   **Compact:** They take up very little storage space.
*   **Easy to clean:** Most reamers are dishwasher safe.

**Cons:**

*   **Requires more effort:** You need to exert more force to extract the juice.
*   **Lower juice yield:** Reamers tend to leave more juice in the fruit compared to other methods.
*   **Messy:** Juice can easily splatter during use.

**Our Verdict:** Handheld reamers are fine for occasional use or if you're on a tight budget. However, for frequent juicing, you'll likely want a more efficient option.

### Press Juicers: Maximum Juice with Minimal Effort

Press juicers, also known as squeezer juicers, use a lever mechanism to press the citrus fruit against a reamer cone. This method extracts more juice with less effort than a handheld reamer.

**Pros:**

*   **High juice yield:** Press juicers are very effective at extracting juice.
*   **Less effort required:** The lever action makes juicing easier, especially for larger quantities.
*   **Less mess:** The enclosed design helps contain splatters.

**Cons:**

*   **More expensive:** Press juicers typically cost between $20 and $50.
*   **Larger footprint:** They take up more storage space than reamers.
*   **Can be difficult to clean:** Some models have multiple parts that need to be disassembled and cleaned.

**Our Verdict:** Press juicers offer a great balance of juice yield, ease of use, and price. They're a good choice for people who juice citrus regularly. Look for models with comfortable handles and dishwasher-safe parts for easy cleaning.

### Lever-Style Juicers: Commercial-Grade Power

Lever-style juicers, often seen in restaurants and bars, are designed for heavy-duty use. They use a powerful lever to press the fruit, extracting a maximum amount of juice.

**Pros:**

*   **Highest juice yield:** Lever juicers are the most efficient at extracting juice.
*   **Durable construction:** These juicers are built to last, often made from cast iron or stainless steel.
*   **Easy to use for large quantities:** The lever action makes juicing large amounts of citrus quick and easy.

**Cons:**

*   **Expensive:** Lever juicers can cost upwards of $100.
*   **Bulky and heavy:** They take up a lot of counter space and can be difficult to move.
*   **Can be difficult to clean:** Some models have intricate designs that are hard to clean thoroughly.

**Our Verdict:** Lever-style juicers are ideal for serious juicers or those who need to juice large quantities of citrus regularly. If you're making lemonade for a crowd or running a bar, this is the type of juicer you need. ## Electric Citrus Juicers: Convenience at a Price

Electric citrus juicers offer the convenience of automated juicing. They typically feature a motorized reamer that spins as you press the fruit against it.

### Basic Electric Juicers: Simple and Affordable

Basic electric juicers are a good entry-level option for those who want the convenience of an electric juicer without breaking the bank.

**Pros:**

*   **Easy to use:** Simply cut the citrus fruit in half and press it onto the spinning reamer.
*   **Faster than manual juicing:** Electric juicers can juice fruit more quickly than manual methods.
*   **Relatively affordable:** Basic models can be found for under $30.

**Cons:**

*   **Can be noisy:** The motor can be quite loud.
*   **Lower juice yield than press juicers:** They may not extract as much juice as a good manual press juicer.
*   **Less durable:** Basic electric juicers tend to be made from less durable materials.

**Our Verdict:** Basic electric juicers are a good choice for occasional use or for those who prioritize convenience over juice yield.

### Automatic Electric Juicers: Hands-Free Juicing

Automatic electric juicers take convenience to the next level. They automatically start juicing when they detect the presence of citrus fruit.

**Pros:**

*   **Hands-free operation:** You don't need to hold the fruit in place.
*   **Efficient juicing:** Many models feature multiple reamer sizes for different types of citrus.
*   **Convenient features:** Some models have pulp control settings and drip-stop spouts.

**Cons:**

*   **More expensive:** Automatic electric juicers can cost upwards of $50.
*   **Can be bulky:** They take up more counter space than basic models.
*   **More difficult to clean:** Some models have intricate designs that are hard to clean thoroughly.

**Our Verdict:** Automatic electric juicers are a good choice for those who juice citrus frequently and want the ultimate in convenience.

## Key Features to Consider When Choosing a Citrus Juicer

No matter which type of citrus juicer you choose, there are several key features to consider:

*   **Juice Yield:** How efficiently does the juicer extract juice from the fruit? Look for models with sharp reamers and efficient pressing mechanisms.
*   **Ease of Use:** Is the juicer easy to assemble, use, and clean? Consider the size and shape of the handles, the ease of pressing or turning the fruit, and the number of parts that need to be cleaned.
*   **Durability:** Is the juicer made from durable materials that will withstand regular use? Look for models made from stainless steel, cast iron, or high-quality plastic.
*   **Stability:** Does the juicer stay stable on the countertop while you're using it? Look for models with non-slip feet or a wide base.
*   **Pulp Control:** Does the juicer have a pulp control feature that allows you to adjust the amount of pulp in the juice? Some people prefer pulp-free juice, while others like a bit of texture.
*   **Capacity:** How much juice can the juicer hold at once? If you're juicing for a large group, you'll want a juicer with a large capacity.
